# Code of conduct

Panalume is a small community project. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or setup.

**Please:**

- be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Home Assistant
- keep discussion about Panalume and how to make it better
- assume good intent, and disagree about ideas, never about people

**Not OK:** harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, or sharing someone's private information.

**What happens:** comments that break these rules may be hidden or removed, and repeated or serious cases may be blocked from the project.

**Reporting:** use **Report content** from the **…** menu on any comment or issue. That goes to GitHub's safety team. For security problems, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
